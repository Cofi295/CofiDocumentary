# Cofi Broadcast Storytelling & Closed-Loop Editorial Mastery

Activate this craft discipline whenever writing documentary scripts, critiquing spoken narration for TTS, planning visual beats, directing Remotion/Vox edits, or executing quality screening across any target country or niche.

## 1. Five Universal Laws of Broadcast Ear-Writing & Single-Narrator TTS

Listeners have only **one chance** to understand a spoken sentence in real time—their ears cannot re-read backwards like eyes on paper. Replace rigid percentage quotas with genuine broadcast ear-writing judgment:

1. **One Breath, One Thought (`일문일의 · 1 Hơi 1 Ý Đơn`)**
   - Carry **one primary idea per breath unit** (`12–18` Korean syllables, `12–18` English/Vietnamese words, or `20–28` Japanese morae per clause).
   - Keep the subject and its active verb close together; never stack 3–4 nested relative clauses before the main verb.
2. **Verbalization Over Nominalization (`동사화 · Verbalstil · Động Từ Hóa`)**
   - Bureaucratic and AI-generated prose freezes action into abstract noun phrases (*"the implementation of an investigation"*, *"~의 발견은"*, *"việc tiến hành kiểm tra"*).
   - Broadcast writers **release action back into concrete active verbs**: nouns sit still; active verbs move the camera inside the listener's mind.
3. **Attribution Before Quotation (`화자 선행 원칙 — Mandatory for Single-Narrator TTS`)**
   - In a **single-narrator documentary (`K1` or any solo VO)**, the listener must know **who is speaking before hearing the words**.
   - Default to **attribution-first indirect or semi-direct narration** (e.g., *"경찰이 출처를 묻자, 그는 짧게 고개를 끄덕이며 시인했습니다"*). Reserve verbatim `"..."` quotes strictly for **2–5 pivotal short statements per episode**, always preceded by explicit speaker framing.
4. **Dynamic Sentence-Length Contrast, Crisp Finite-Verb Dramatic Brakes & Strict Ban on Trailing Noun-Fragment Echoes (`장단 호흡 대비 · 초단문 동사 브레이크 우선 · 문단 끝 체언 종결 동어반복 태그 전면 금지`)**
   - Never write uniform `40–50` character conveyor-belt sentences. Deploy crisp short dramatic brakes (`초단문 브레이크`, `<= 16` non-space chars) at action impacts, timestamps, and twists—using **crisp short finite-verb sentences** (`증거는 사라졌습니다.`, `답은 하나뿐이었습니다.`, `시계는 멈춰 섰죠.`).
   - **Strictly ban trailing verbless noun-fragment echoes (`문단 끝 체언 종결 요약 태그`) at the end of paragraphs** that merely repeat or summarize the sentence right before them (e.g., `단 여섯 평의 철제 방.`, `칠천육백일흔 날의 은신.`, `끊어진 전화선 한 가닥.`, `강제 침입 흔적 영 건.`, `반쪽 장문과 사십이 호 작업화.`, `단칸방에 남겨진 신분증 한 장.`, `방수포 아래의 어둠.`, `창살 없는 고철 감옥.`, `스물한 번의 겨울.`, `단 하나의 표적.`, `버려진 종이컵 하나.`, `99.99퍼센트의 일치.`, `열여섯 개 특징점의 일치.`, `법정 최고형 확정.`). Every paragraph ending must close on a natural finite predicate (`~습니다`, `~었죠`, `~거든요`, `~겁니다`, or `?`), and standalone 1-line `체언 종결` paragraphs (`standalone_one_line_noun_crutch_excess`) are strictly forbidden.
5. **Audio-Visual Counterpoint & Meaningful Silence (`Walter Murch + 間/留白`)**
   - **Never narrate the literal surface of what the viewer already sees** (*"See dog, don't say dog"*). Let picture show physical evidence while narration reveals hidden causality or timeline paradox, giving major revelations **1.2s–2.8s of breathing room**.

## 2. Country-Specific Broadcast Prose & Anti-Translationese Mastery

### 🇰🇷 Korea (`ko` — SBS *그것이 알고 싶다 / 꼬꼬무* · KBS *다큐 인사이트*)
- **100% Broadcast Green-Dollar Writing Craft (`유튜브 수익창출 100% 녹색 달러 심의 화법`)**:
  - **60-Second Diamond Clean Zone (`00:00–01:00` / First 6–8 Sentences)**: `0%` death, corpse, skeletal remains, blood, weapon, physical assault, suicide, or gore terms (`살인/살해/사망/숨진/시신/사체/백골/피/혈흔/총/총성/칼/흉기/폭행/자살/성폭행/학대` = `0%`). Hook viewers purely through physical/ledger anomalies, mysterious vanishings, alibi contradictions, or double-life masks.
  - **Full-Script Broadcast Forensic Refraction (`ACT01..ACT05`)**: `0%` direct depiction of violent physical mechanics (stabbing, shooting, strangling, torture), corpse/decomposition states, sexual crimes, child abuse, or suicide methods. Refract serious outcomes into calm investigative/judicial phrasing (`"그날 밤 이후 행적이 완전히 끊겼습니다"`, `"돌이킬 수 없는 비극 직후"`, `"현장에서 확보된 결정적 미세 시료"`, `"법정 최고형이 확정되었습니다"`).
- **Balanced Sentence-Ending Music (`~습니다 58%–72%` · Zero Pendulum Extremes · Zero Trailing Noun-Fragment Echoes)**: Keep active verbal `~습니다 / ~었습니다` in the **`58%–72%`** range (max **4 consecutive** before varying!), weaving observational `~었죠 / ~았죠 / ~였죠` (`10%–16%`, never back-to-back), conversational insider explanations `~거든요` (`3%–6%`, `5–12` times/episode), causal reveals `~겁니다 / ~것입니다 / ~셈입니다` (`5%–9%`), genuine investigative questions `?` (`4%–8%`), and crisp short finite-verb dramatic brakes (`<= 16` non-space chars: `증거는 사라졌습니다.`, `답은 하나뿐이었습니다.`, `시계는 멈춰 섰죠.`). **Never append a verbless noun fragment (`체언 종결`) at the end of a paragraph to repeat/echo the preceding sentence** (`단 여섯 평의 철제 방.`, `끊어진 전화선 한 가닥.`, `99.99퍼센트의 일치.`, `법정 최고형 확정.` = `0%`).
- **Zero Hanging Broadcast Endings, Zero MC Meta-Transitions & Zero Archaic `하오체`**: Strictly ban 1st-person `~더군요` (`0%`), cap standalone period-terminated `~는데요.` at `0–2` sentences (`<= 1%`), ban MC-style meta-transition announcements (`"그날 새벽의 ... 도로로 시선을 옮겨 보겠습니다"`, `"지금부터 하나씩 따라가 보겠습니다"`, `"자, 그럼 ... 살펴보겠습니다"` = `0%` — transition directly via concrete scene/time anchors), and ban period-drama `하오체` (`"맞소"`, `"처음이오"`, `"같소"`) in modern topics.
- **Anti-Formulaic Prose Hygiene**:
  - **Zero Police-Blotter Person Tags**: Never repeat `[Age + Occupation + Name]` (`마흔여덟 살의 모 사장`, `스물여섯 살 청년 천쉰민`); introduce people via action/scene and state age only once when forensically meaningful.
  - **Action-Camera Subject Flow**: In pursuit/raid/inspection sequences, shift subjects to sounds, locks, lights, and motion rather than repeating `[Role] [Name] 씨는/씨가` across consecutive sentences.
  - **Zero PowerPoint Listicle Skeletons**: Never write `첫째는 ...였습니다, 둘째는 ...였습니다, 셋째는 ...였습니다`; weave multi-part mechanisms organically through narrative transitions.
  - **Zero Artificial Micro-Stopwatch Padding & Zero Post-Verdict Loops**: Avoid fake `0.05초 / 0.1초` padding; after final court sentencing in Act 5, close crisply within 4–8 aftermath/retention-bridge sentences without looping back to recap the 20-year manhunt.
- **TTS-Ready Pure Hangul Orthography in `script.md`**:
  - Never use `[Numeral] + 해` for elapsed years (`열여덟 해` sounds like `18회` in TTS -> always write **`18년`**, **`19년`**, **`24년`**).
  - Never mix Sino-Korean thousands/hundreds with Native-Korean day counters (`칠천육백일흔 날` X -> always write standard Sino-Korean **`7,670일` (`칠천육백칠십 일`)** O).
  - Never insert a space inside generation prefixes (`이 세대 전자 주민신분증` X -> TTS misreads `이` as "this"; always write **`2세대 전자 주민신분증` (`이세대`)** O), and never write redundant `일천` before hundreds (`일천이백여 곳` X -> always write **`천이백여 곳` (`1,200여 곳`)** O).
  - Write `'퍼센트'` (never `'%'`), write `1..20` before Native-Korean counters in **spaced Hangul words** (`서류 두 장`, `열쇠 세 개`, `여덟 개`, `단서 아홉 점`, `공범 네 명`, `통화 여섯 번` — never `10개, 2장, 8개, 9점`), write `백여만 위안` (never `100여만 위안`), expand license plates / leading-zero codes / ASCII letters / numeric model codes into **pure Hangul** (`웨 에이알 공칠사칠 호`, `양산 공구오 호`, `일급 수배령`, `오륙식`, `팔일식`, `오사식`, `백사십칠 연대` — zero raw `[A-Za-z]` or `0747호` or `56식`), replace `0건` with `단 한 건도 없었습니다` or `영 건` (preventing `영건 -> 연건`), and transliterate Chinese `谢 (Xie)` names strictly as **`시에`** (`시에쭝펀`, `우시에위`, `시에 씨` — `0` `셰`).

### 🇺🇸🇬🇧 English (`en` — PBS *Frontline* · CBS *60 Minutes* · BBC · *LEMMiNO* · *Vox*)
- Strip front-loaded participial clauses (*"Having evaded police across eight provinces..."* -> *"For eight years, he slipped past police across eight provinces."*).
- Ban AI clichés: *delve into, testament to, tapestry, stark reminder, chillingly, little did they know, not only X but also Y, it is worth noting, underscore*.
- Use **NPR Friendly Numbers** in spoken prose when exact cents are not forensic clues, while keeping exact dates/amounts on Vox cards.

### 🇻🇳 Vietnam (`vi` — VTV1 *Phóng Sự Điều Tra* · *VTV Đặc Biệt*)
- Balance **thanh bằng (ngang/huyền)** and **thanh trắc** at clause endings so TTS never chokes on 4–5 consecutive heavy tones.
- Eliminate translationese (*"tiến hành thực hiện sự kiểm tra"*, *"bị phát hiện bởi"*, *"không chỉ... mà còn..."*) in favor of crisp Vietnamese active verbs (*"lần theo", "khép vòng vây", "phơi bày", "bốc hơi"*).

### 🇯🇵 Japan (`ja` — *NHKスペシャル / 映像の世紀*)
- Avoid Kanji homophone traps (`同音異義語`: `私立/市立`, `科学/化学`) in spoken narration; prefer clear **Yamato-kotoba (`大和言葉`)** for the ear and avoid repetitive `〜のである / 〜のだ` walls.

### 🇩🇪 DE · 🇫🇷 FR · 🇪🇸 ES · 🇨🇳 CN
- **DE (`ARD/ZDF`)**: Verbalstil over bureaucratic `Nominalstil`; avoid 30-word `Schachtelsätze` that bury the verb.
- **FR (`ARTE`)**: Break `qui... que... dont...` chains into tactile, short radio-documentary sentences.
- **ES (`RTVE`)**: Active verbs and historical present over `fue + participio` passive chains.
- **CN (`CCTV`)**: Disyllabic clarity (`听觉语言`), micro-action verbs (`动词化`), and restrained emotional space (`留白`) without moralizing sermons.

## 3. Six Niche Retention Engines, 8 Cold-Open Hook Archetypes & 5 Case-Driven Narrative Lenses

1. **True Crime, Vault Forensics & Fugitive Psychology (5-Act Backbone `ACT01 -> ACT02 -> ACT03 -> ACT04 -> ACT05` + 8 Cold-Open Hook Archetypes + 5 Case-Driven Narrative Lenses + Cold-Open Non-Spoiler & Sibling-Case Contrast Bridge Laws)**:
   - **8 Topic-Adaptive Cold-Open Hook Archetypes (`0%` Single-Formula Opening, `0%` Arrest/Capture Spoiler, `0%` Premature Real-Name Leak)**: Adapt the opening `00:00–01:00` to the anatomy of each topic (`micro_forensic_anomaly`, `double_life_unmasking`, `chilling_verbatim_line`, `countdown_system_breach`, `locked_room_vault_paradox`, `last_ordinary_routine`, `scale_absurdity_collision`, `cold_archive_match` — never copy `[Year/City]... 하지만...`). **Never spoil the final arrest raid/capture date (`2020년 10월 22일` cuffing/raid) or prematurely leak the fugitive's real name (`쉬다펑`) inside the Cold Open (`00:00–02:00`) when building an anonymous alias mystery hook ("the man known only as Li Gang who lived 7,670 days without ID, bank account, or hospital visits")**—keep the arrest raid exclusively once in the Act 4/Act 5 climax and pivot from the Cold-Open suspense question directly into the original crime scene (`ACT01`). Maintain 100% Broadcast Green-Dollar safety (zero death/corpse/blood/weapon words in `00:00–01:00` and indirect forensic/judicial refraction throughout).
   - **Closing Hook (`Sibling-Case Retention Bridge Outro` at end of `ACT05` — `0%` Generic Channel-Promo Filler)**: Strictly ban generic channel-advertisement lines (`"우리 채널에 기록된 다음 수사 파일에서 확인해 보시기 바랍니다"`, `"다음 영상에서 찾아뵙겠습니다"`, `"구독과 좋아요..."`). When `outro_mode == "sibling_case_retention_bridge"`, close by contrasting the protagonist's modus operandi directly against a specific real sibling case on the channel (e.g., contrasting a nameless ghost with zero ID against an elite fugitive like `우시에위` who used 30 forged IDs to wear a thousand faces) and end with an open curiosity question (`"...기괴한 도주극은 또 어떤 결말을 맞이했을까요?"`).
   - Anchor every episode on the canonical **5-Act Structure (`ACT01 -> ACT02 -> ACT03 -> ACT04 -> ACT05`)** while selecting the **Case-Driven Narrative Lens (5 Lăng Kính Tự Sự Linh Hoạt Theo Đặc Thù Vụ Án)** best suited to the anatomy of the case:
     1. `Forensic Trace Reverse-Reconstruction` (Giải phẫu Ngược từ Vật chứng & Dị điểm Hiện trường)
     2. `Dual-Timeline Cat & Mouse` (Song hành 2 Trục Thời Gian: Dấu vết Quá khứ vs. Truy vết Hiện tại)
     3. `Rashomon / Multi-Witness Contradiction` (Đối chiếu Lời khai Mâu thuẫn & Điểm mù Nhân chứng)
     4. `Systemic Web & Audit Trail` (Giải phẫu Mạng lưới Tài chính / Kho bạc / Lỗ hổng Hệ thống)
     5. `Psychological Interrogation Duel` (Đấu trí Tâm lý trong Phòng Thẩm vấn & Phá vỡ Alibi)
2. **Cyber, Finance & Business Forensics**: Anchor huge sums against **one human passbook/family impact** -> trace **Follow-the-Money mechanisms** with concrete visual metaphors -> never dump >2 consecutive raw number sentences without human translation.
3. **Declassified History, Archives & Geopolitics**: Open inside the decision room (timestamped memo/tape/map line) -> contrast **Public Rhetoric vs. Declassified Record** -> treat geography/maps as active causal forces.
4. **Mega-Infrastructure, Engineering & Disaster**: Open at the physical threshold breach -> trace the **Physical Domino Chain (`Geology/Material -> Human Trade-off -> Micro-fracture -> Systemic Cascade`)**.
5. **Spiritual, Pastoral & 60-Minute Night Letters**: Open with gentle 2nd-person empathy -> pace via **8–10 minute Wave-Breathing movements** (empathy -> parable/Scripture -> quiet release -> blessing) with open vowels and zero preachiness.
6. **Deep Society, Medicine & Human Interest**: **Micro-to-Macro-to-Micro Lens** (one lived human moment -> systemic structural root -> quiet human echo; *Show, Don't Moralize*).

## 4. Closed-Loop Production Protocol (`Maker -> Sensor -> Critic -> Fixer -> Final`)

Every production stage must operate as a verified closed loop rather than a single blind pass:
1. **Maker**: Authors the artifact with clean craft context and zero contradictory quotas.
2. **Deterministic Sensor**: Measures concrete physical reality first (prose cadence/repetition metrics, Whisper ASR + acoustic edges on `.wav`, 3-frame OpenCV decode on media, coordinate safe-zone & frame-continuity checks on Remotion edits, `320x180` mobile legibility on thumbnails, `ffprobe` + EBU R128 + outro speech tail on MP4).
3. **Independent Critic**: Reviews actual output + sensor telemetry in a fresh, unbiased context and emits **addressed defect tickets** (`line` or `beat_id` + root cause + exact fix direction).
4. **Surgical Fixer**: Repairs only the defective lines/beats/chunks without corrupting verified surroundings, then re-runs Sensor verification until `FINAL`.
